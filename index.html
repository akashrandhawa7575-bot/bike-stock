Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer loan or finance options?

Yes, we can help connect you with local financing partners to make purchasing your next bike easier. Contact us on WhatsApp for exact EMI details based on the bike you choose.

How long does the RC transfer take?

We handle all the paperwork for you! The RC transfer process typically takes 15-30 days depending on the local RTO processing times, but you can ride the bike immediately with the transfer receipt.

Can I sell my bike to you directly?

Absolutely. We buy bikes directly with outright cash payments and handle the RC transfer hassle-free. Just bring your bike in for a same-day inspection.
